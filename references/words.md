# Words and phrases

Use this during the check pass. Reading it before drafting primes the words.

- A word on this list is a reason to look closer, not a ban. One "crucial" in 800 words is fine. Three list words in one paragraph mean the paragraph was written on autopilot, so rewrite the paragraph.
- Swapping a flagged word for a synonym rarely fixes anything. Ask what the sentence is trying to say, then say that.
- Leave technical senses alone: "robust" in statistics, "leverage" in finance, "key" in cryptography.
- Word habits change with each model release. If an unlisted word shows up three times in one piece, treat it as if it were listed.

## Stock AI vocabulary

These appear far more often in model text than in human text from before 2023.

| Words | Try |
|---|---|
| delve into, embark on, navigate (figurative) | look at, start, handle; or just start |
| tapestry, mosaic, symphony, myriad, plethora, a diverse array of | the number, or name the parts |
| testament to, underscore, highlight, showcase | show; or state the fact |
| pivotal, crucial, vital, key, paramount | important, main; or show why |
| robust, comprehensive, meticulous, nuanced | strong, complete, careful; or the actual property |
| leverage, utilize, harness | use |
| facilitate, foster, cultivate, bolster | help, build, support |
| enhance, elevate, streamline | improve, simplify; or say how |
| realm, landscape, ecosystem | the field, the market; or name it |
| multifaceted, intricate, interplay | complicated, details, how X affects Y |
| seamless, transformative, groundbreaking, remarkable | what changed, by how much |
| unveil, garner | release, get |
| journey, endeavor, cornerstone, beacon, legacy | process, project; or the actual role |
| genuinely, truly, deeply, incredibly, quietly | cut, or back it with a fact |
| additionally, furthermore, moreover, notably | also, and; or nothing |

## Corporate and marketing words

unlock, empower, supercharge, revolutionize, reimagine, game-changer, cutting-edge, best-in-class, world-class, solutions, offerings, synergy, move the needle, deep dive, double down, lean into, circle back, learnings, actionable insights, drive (growth), impactful.

Replace each with the specific result, the product's name, or a number. "Change [metric] by [amount]" beats "move the needle."

## Phrases that delay the point

Delete them and keep whatever follows.

- It's worth noting that / It's important to remember / Needless to say
- When it comes to / In terms of / With regard to / In order to (use "to")
- At its core / At the end of the day / The reality is
- In today's fast-paced world / In an era of / In the ever-evolving world of
- As mentioned earlier / As we'll see / Let's dive in / Let's break it down
- That being said / With that in mind / Moving forward
- The key takeaway is / The bottom line is
