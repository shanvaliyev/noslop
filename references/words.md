# Words and phrases

Use this during the check pass. Reading it before drafting primes the words.

How to use it:

- A word on this list is a signal to look closer. One "crucial" in 800 words is fine. Three list words in one paragraph means the paragraph was written on autopilot, so rewrite the paragraph.
- Replacing a flagged word with a synonym in the same sentence rarely fixes anything. Ask what the sentence is trying to say, then say that.
- Leave technical senses alone: "robust" in statistics, "leverage" in finance, "landscape" in a painting, "key" in cryptography.
- Word habits change with each model release, so check structure (see patterns.md) first.

## Brochure words

nestled, in the heart of, boasts, breathtaking, stunning, vibrant, picturesque, charming, rich (heritage, history, culture), hidden gem, must-visit, curated, handpicked, bespoke, artisanal, lovingly crafted, a feast for the senses, something for everyone.

Fix: say what's there, who made it, and what it costs. "The bakery opens at 6 and sells out of rye by 9" beats "a charming artisanal bakery."

## Phrases that delay the point

Delete them and keep whatever follows.

- It's worth noting that / It's important to note / It's important to remember
- When it comes to
- At its core / At the end of the day / The reality is / The truth is
- In today's fast-paced world / In today's digital age / In an era of / In the ever-evolving world of
- In the realm of / In the world of / In the landscape of
- In order to (use "to") / Due to the fact that (use "because") / In terms of / With regard to
- Needless to say / It goes without saying
- As mentioned earlier / As we'll see / As you can see
- Let's dive in / Let's explore / Let's break it down / Let's unpack
- Without further ado / Buckle up
- Going forward / Moving forward
- That being said / With that in mind / All in all
- The key takeaway is / The bottom line is

## Hedges

Tier 3 alone, a problem when stacked: could potentially, might possibly, may arguably, it could be argued that, to some extent, in some cases, it seems that, somewhat, relatively, fairly, generally speaking, tends to.

Keep one when the doubt is real and the source supports it.

## Era note

Different models lean on different words, and the list moves. Rough guide from Wikipedia's AI cleanup project:

- 2023 to mid-2024: delve, tapestry, testament, additionally, boasts, crucial, enduring, garner, interplay, key, landscape, meticulous, valuable.
- Mid-2024 to mid-2025: align with, enhance, fostering, highlighting, showcasing.
- 2025 onward: emphasizing, highlighting, quietly, genuinely, load-bearing, "full stop," "worth stating plainly," "the honest answer is," and notability talk ("featured in," "independent coverage").

If a word you've never seen flagged shows up three times in one piece, treat it like it's on this list.
